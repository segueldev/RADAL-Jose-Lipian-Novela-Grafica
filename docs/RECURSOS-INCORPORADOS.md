## Mano con anticuchos

Modo: herramienta integrada de generación/edición de imágenes, fondo transparente. Referencia: anticucho.png del usuario. Se añadió una mano para sostener el recurso en las poses cuyos brazos no aparecen; las caras originales no se editaron. Maxi usa sus propias manos mediante una composición.

Archivo guardado: `Radal-1.2-linux/game/images/eventos/anticucho_mano.png`.

Prompt final:

> Use case: compositing. Asset type: transparent photographic prop sprite for a Chilean Ren'Py visual novel. Input image 1 is the EDIT TARGET: preserve the supplied three barbecue anticucho skewers and grilled meat faithfully, with the same meat texture and relative arrangement, no replacement food. Add only ONE realistic light-to-medium olive skin human hand grasping the bare wooden ends below all meat, with a short wrist emerging from a plain BLACK jacket cuff. The hand comes into view from the lower edge; the skewers point up. A relaxed natural grip with correct anatomy and no additional fingers. Entire prop isolated on a genuinely transparent background, no scene, no person, no face, no writing, no watermark. Vertical composition with the meat and skewers occupying upper two thirds and the gripping hand and black cuff occupying lower third. Keep all three meat sticks visible and unobscured. Clean alpha edges, photographic realism and diffuse daylight matching the provided photo. Do not include a tabletop or plate.

Los recortes del cuy preparado y cocinado se mantienen como recursos proporcionados por el usuario.

WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9 composition, eye-level camera, diffuse daylight. Small ordinary kitchen, simple cupboards, worn but clean surfaces, no luxury decor. The fridge is grounded on the tiled floor, door closed, entirely visible in the upper right two thirds. A wooden kitchen table occupies the foreground, its empty horizontal tabletop clearly visible across the lower half of the image. Leave the CENTER of the tabletop EMPTY for a food sprite added by the game. No food, no animals, no people, no writing, no captions, no watermark. Keep the main setting above the lower 25 percent because the game dialogue overlays there. Natural photographic realism. No illustrated or cartoon elements.

A believable retail toy box, blue and red cardboard edges and clear front showing a little robot figure, no text or brand logos. Keep the right-side peace-sign hand unchanged. Do not add a second hand or change his face. Preserve the transparent background and entire visible torso, clean alpha edges.
